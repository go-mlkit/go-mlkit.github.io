What go-mlkit will cover

machine learning, in pure Go.

go-mlkit is the planned home of machine learning for go-scikit: clustering, classification and model selection, the parts of MATLAB's Statistics and Machine Learning Toolbox and Mathematica's FindClusters, Classify and Predict that no go-scikit organisation covers. It is in wave 3, together with go-statistics time series (PLAN.md item 15). Everything below is planned, not released.

◎

Clustering

k-means with k-means++ seeding, hierarchical clustering, DBSCAN, Gaussian mixtures fitted by EM.

🌳

Trees and forests

Decision trees, random forests and gradient boosting, from Breiman and Friedman.

⟂

SVM and linear models

SMO and dual coordinate descent in the style of LIBSVM and LIBLINEAR; logistic regression and k-NN.

📉

PCA and factor analysis

Dimension reduction and rotations, the pca and factoran of MATLAB.

✓

Model selection

Cross-validation splitters, grid search and metrics.

🧪

Validated against scikit-learn

With the same seeds and initialisation the partitions must equal scikit-learn's; metrics are compared to cross-validation noise.

📦

Pure Go, CGO=0

No cgo; the same on every architecture the fleet tests.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

scikit-learn, LIBSVM and LIBLINEAR are BSD-3 and can be ported; GPL tools are oracles only.
